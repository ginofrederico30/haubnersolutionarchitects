Services

NetSuite work, scoped to what your operation actually needs.

Five service lines cover the full lifecycle — from first implementation to years of ongoing optimization. Every engagement starts with a scoping call, not a generic package.

Implementations

Full-lifecycle NetSuite deployment: requirements discovery, chart of accounts and role design, module configuration, data load, UAT, and go-live support. Typical engagements run [X–X weeks] depending on module scope and entity count.

  • Financials, order management, inventory, and CRM modules configured to your workflows

  • Multi-entity and multi-subsidiary structures, including intercompany and consolidation

  • Role-based security and approval workflows built for audit readiness

What's included

  • Discovery & requirements workshop
  • Solution design document
  • Configuration & data migration
  • UAT support & go-live cutover
  • [X weeks] hypercare period

What's included

  • Legacy system & data audit
  • Data cleansing & mapping
  • Parallel-run reconciliation
  • Historical data load
  • Cutover runbook

Migrations

Moving off QuickBooks, a legacy ERP, or a patchwork of spreadsheets carries real risk to financial accuracy. We migrate historical data, reconcile balances line-by-line, and run a parallel period before cutover — so nothing gets lost in the switch.

  • Chart of accounts remapping and historical GL migration

  • Open transaction migration (AR/AP, POs, inventory on hand)

  • Reconciled parallel-run before legacy system shutdown

Customizations

When out-of-the-box NetSuite doesn't match a workflow, we build to it — SuiteScript for custom logic, SuiteFlow for approval routing, custom records and forms for the edge cases your business actually has. Documented, versioned, and built to survive upgrades.

  • SuiteScript 2.x development (client, user event, scheduled, Map/Reduce)

  • Custom workflows, records, forms, and saved search reporting

  • Technical documentation handed off with every build

What's included

  • Requirements & technical design
  • Sandbox build & testing
  • Production deployment
  • Handoff documentation

Common integrations

  • Salesforce & other CRMs
  • Shopify & ecommerce platforms
  • Payroll & HRIS systems
  • Warehouse & 3PL systems
  • EDI & banking feeds

Integrations

NetSuite rarely runs alone. We build and maintain integrations to the CRM, ecommerce, payroll, and warehouse systems your operation depends on — using SuiteTalk, RESTlets, or a middleware platform, chosen based on volume and reliability requirements, not default habit.

Ongoing Support

A go-live isn't the finish line. Support retainers cover release management, user training, troubleshooting, and incremental optimization as your processes evolve — so the system that fit on day one still fits two years later.

  • Monthly or block-hour retainer options

  • NetSuite release readiness reviews (twice yearly)

  • Direct access to the consultant who built your system — no ticket queue

Also available

  • NetSuite health check / instance audit
  • Ad hoc project work
  • Training sessions for new hires
